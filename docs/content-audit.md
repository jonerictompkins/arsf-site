# Public-site content audit

Audit date: 2026-07-26

## What is working today

- ARSF's mission and decades of expertise are unusually strong source material.
- The original heart-and-Akita logo is memorable and remains the core identity; the old site also uses a translucent version as a repeating background.
- Available dogs are maintained with photos and direct Petfinder profile links.
- Square and PayPal donation routes exist.
- The site contains substantial Akita education, owner-support, rehoming, memorial, and success-story material.
- Happy Tails, Dog Stars, picnic galleries, videos, and individual memorial pages form a meaningful historical archive.
- The site links to a broader national Akita rescue network, giving ARSF a useful role as both local service and breed resource.
- Recent homepage update dates show the website is still actively maintained.

## Friction observed

- Fixed-position desktop CSS and legacy Spry scripts make responsive navigation and small-screen use fragile.
- The homepage presents dozens of similarly weighted links, images, appeals, partner logos, notices, and memorial items.
- The primary Petfinder search link includes a Sarasota location context and returned no visible results during the audit, even though individual ARSF Petfinder listings were live.
- Older application routes remain discoverable, but the current Forms page now promotes an ARSF Jotform application.
- Important journeys—adopt, donate, foster, rehome, get breed guidance, and contact—compete rather than forming a clear hierarchy.
- Frequently changed content is embedded directly in HTML, so visual editing and content editing are inseparable.

## Prototype priorities

1. Present ARSF as a society devoted to the whole life and betterment of the breed.
2. Make education, owner support, rescue, placement, celebration, and remembrance equally discoverable.
3. Preserve and promote the original ARSF logo instead of repeating it as visual texture.
4. Give editors structured fields instead of page-layout controls.
5. Keep sensitive adoption applications out of the website repository.
6. Migrate the long-form educational and historical archive collection by collection.

## Content provenance

The first-pass organization copy is adapted from public ARSF pages. Featured-dog facts and descriptions are condensed from public ARSF Petfinder listings. Prototype images were copied from ARSF's public website and public Petfinder listings for this independent review build. The exact ARSF logo was preserved from `Images/arsf_logo230x200.fw.png`; archive previews currently use Hisashi from Happy Tails, Magnolia from Dog Stars, and ARSF's Rainbow Bridge art. ARSF should approve copy, photos, current dog status, and usage rights before this becomes an official site.
