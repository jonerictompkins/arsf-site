{{mission}}

ARSF combines adoption with education, owner support, responsible rehoming guidance, spay and neuter assistance, medical care, and lifelong help for adopters. The goal is not simply to move dogs—it is to create placements that last.

Responsible placementApplications, conversations, home visits, and deliberate introductions.
Complete careHealth screening, spay or neuter, microchipping, and treatment as needed.
Lifetime supportPractical Akita experience remains available after adoption day.
