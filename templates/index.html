{{mission}}

Dorie Sparkman and ARSF’s volunteers have built more than a placement organization. Their work carries decades of breed experience, direct support for owners and shelters, medical advocacy, careful matching, and enduring relationships with adopters.

Breed bettermentEducation, responsible ownership, spay and neuter support, and practical guidance.
Thoughtful rescueMedical care, temperament knowledge, deliberate placement, and accountability.
